2. Payout engine Checking

Every test payout from the testnet run. The engine checks each request against the mandate before paying, then records a receipt: task, recipient, amount and transaction. The last column asks Stellar testnet, live, whether that transaction really happened.

3. MCP server Checking

MCP is the standard way AI assistants call outside tools. With the UJIRA server added, an assistant such as Claude Desktop or Cursor can pay people from a mandate, and the contract still decides what is allowed.

The four tools

  • quote_payout

    Works out what a payout would cost and whether the mandate would allow it. Nothing is paid.

  • request_payout

    Asks the mandate to pay a recipient for a task. Over a limit it is refused; at or above the approval amount it waits for a person to approve.

  • payout_status

    Says where a payout stands: waiting for approval, paid or refused, with its transaction on Stellar.

  • list_receipts

    Lists the receipts for paid payouts: task, recipient, amount and transaction hash.
